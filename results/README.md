# Main-experiment result summaries

- `table1_headline_results.csv`: headline FNSPID results for the twelve main models at the three VaR levels.
- `table2_dm_hac.csv`: DM-HAC summary for daily cross-sectional mean pinball-loss differences.
- `table18_dependence_inference.csv`: pooled breach-rate point estimates, moving-block intervals, and DQ results.
- `table19_daily_summary.csv`: pooled breach rates plus raw-daily dispersion, stress maxima, and lag-1 autocorrelation.

Only headline main-experiment results and the temporal diagnostics requested during review are included. Ablations, task-setting sensitivities, backbone controls, Astock results, and case-study outputs are excluded.

These files are summary outputs. The complete 986-date daily breach and daily mean pinball-loss series needed for independent end-to-end recomputation are still pending export.
